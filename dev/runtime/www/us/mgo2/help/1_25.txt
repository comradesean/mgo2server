COMMUNITY SUPPORT

This server is a private, non-commercial revival of METAL GEAR
ONLINE. It is not operated by or affiliated with Konami.

The original support channels no longer exist, and the addresses
printed in the game's manual will not reach anyone.

For help with this server, contact the operator directly.

Reporting a problem is most useful when it includes:

  - your character name
  - the screen you were on
  - what you expected to happen
  - the error code, if one was shown

Thank you for playing.
